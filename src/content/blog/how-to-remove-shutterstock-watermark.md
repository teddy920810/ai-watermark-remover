Trying to remove Shutterstock watermark from an image? First check which file you have. A large Shutterstock logo or repeating diagonal text usually appears on a preview—not on the licensed download. The correct fix for that preview is to obtain the licensed, unwatermarked file, not to edit around the logo.

If you already own the image or have permission to modify a separate file with an unwanted overlay, an online AI cleanup tool or Photoshop may help. This guide explains how to remove a watermark from Shutterstock-related project files in the appropriate situation, what each method can actually do, and how to check the result.

## Why Do Shutterstock Images Have Watermarks?

Shutterstock provides watermarked “Try” or comp images so designers can evaluate placement, crop, and composition before choosing an asset. Its Help Center says the unwatermarked image is available through licensing. In late 2025, Shutterstock also described an updated watermark design, including contributor attribution on some large previews; the exact pattern may differ by image.

![Illustrative scene: A paper image comp with a visible preview watermark before licensing. Not a screenshot of the named software or evidence of a completed product test.](/uploads/how-to-remove-shutterstock-watermark/image3.jpeg)

A watermark is therefore more than a visual defect. It indicates a preview workflow and helps protect contributors. Erasing visible pixels does not reveal the authentic hidden image, change the Shutterstock license, or create permission to publish the file. Standard and Enhanced licenses support different uses, particularly for merchandise, templates, and high-volume print projects.

Official guidance: [Preview and image licensing](https://www.shutterstock.com/help/en/articles/10617030-can-i-get-comp-images-or-clips); see also the image detail and license explanations in Sources.

## Method 1: Download the Shutterstock Image Without a Watermark

This is the recommended solution whenever you want the actual Shutterstock stock asset. It preserves the original image quality and puts the licensing decision in the correct workflow.

![Illustrative scene: An illustrative image purchase and download screen on a desktop monitor. Not a screenshot of the named software or evidence of a completed product test.](/uploads/how-to-remove-shutterstock-watermark/image4.jpeg)

Step 1. Find the image. Open the Shutterstock asset detail page from your saved link, collection, or image ID.

Step 2. Select Download (not Try). The Try action provides a watermarked preview for evaluation.

Step 3. Review the size, available plan, and license. Check whether the intended usage requires a Standard, Enhanced, or another applicable agreement.

Step 4. Complete the licensing/download process in the authorized account. Save the downloaded file and relevant license record.

Step 5. Replace the placeholder in your design. Check the new file at full resolution, then verify the crop, placement, and any text overlay in your layout.

### Already Paid, but the Shutterstock Watermark Is Still There?

A common cause is that the project still references the earlier Try preview or a cached thumbnail. Locate the file downloaded from the licensed account; compare its filename, resolution, and asset ID with the image in the design. If a teammate purchased it, check the account and applicable license arrangements rather than assuming the asset can be transferred. If the true licensed download itself still contains a Shutterstock watermark, contact Shutterstock support with the image ID and transaction details.

## Method 3: How to Remove a Watermark in Photoshop

Photoshop gives you more manual control for an image you have the right to modify. Its Remove tool can fill a selected region from nearby visual information and may use generative AI, depending on the selected Mode. It still cannot confer a Shutterstock license or recover the original pixels exactly.

![Illustrative scene: A photographer retouching a product image on a desktop computer. Not a screenshot of the named software or evidence of a completed product test.](/uploads/how-to-remove-shutterstock-watermark/image9.jpeg)

Step 1. Open an authorized image in Photoshop. Duplicate the image layer or work on a new layer so you can compare your edit with the original.

Step 2. Choose the Remove tool from the toolbar. If it is hidden, look under the related retouching tools.

Step 3. Set the brush slightly larger than the unwanted overlay, or draw a loop around the region. Keep the affected area as small as practical.

Step 4. In the Options bar, choose an available Mode such as Auto, Generative AI on, or Generative AI off according to the project and device. Depending on your version, these controls may also appear in the Contextual Task Bar.

Step 5. Apply the edit and inspect the result at 100% zoom. Repeat in smaller passes for seams, repeating patterns, thin lines, or complicated details.

Step 6. Save a layered working copy, then export the finished image in the file format you need. Recheck all usage rights before publication.

## What to Check After AI or Photoshop Watermark Cleanup

Before you export an edited image, compare the result with the original at actual size. The main risk is not just a remaining watermark—it is that AI may invent an incorrect replacement for the detail underneath.

![WatermarkGemini original image preview and Original Result Compare controls](/uploads/how-to-remove-shutterstock-watermark/image10.png)

- Faces, hands, and products: inspect skin texture, logos, labels, and fine outlines for invented features.
- Architecture, grids, and fabric: check for misaligned straight lines and mismatched repeated patterns.
- Color and resolution: make sure the repaired area does not have a soft patch, color shift, or compression halo.
- Rights and provenance: retain the original, any applicable license record, and a clean working copy for review.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can you remove a Shutterstock watermark without a license?

Image editors may change visible pixels, but that does not give you rights to publish or distribute an unlicensed Shutterstock preview. For the stock image itself, license and download the original from Shutterstock. Only edit a separate image when you have the needed rights and permission.

### Why does my Shutterstock image still show a watermark after I download it?

You may have saved the Try preview rather than the licensed download, or your design may still link to an old local copy. Check the account, asset ID, source path, and resolution. If the properly licensed file itself is marked, contact Shutterstock with the image ID.

### Can AI completely remove a repeated Shutterstock watermark?

There is no reliable quality guarantee. AI inpainting predicts replacement pixels rather than recovering the original image hidden beneath repeated lettering. A complicated mark across faces or fine detail can leave blur or artificial patterns; the licensed original is preferable.

### Do I need to sign in to use WatermarkGemini?

### Can I crop out the Shutterstock watermark?

Cropping can change the visible composition of an authorized image, but it does not change the rights attached to a Shutterstock preview. If you need that stock photo for final use, license it rather than relying on a cropped preview.

### Do I need a Standard or Enhanced Shutterstock license?

It depends on use. Shutterstock describes Standard licenses for many ordinary digital projects, with additional restrictions; Enhanced licenses add rights for uses such as merchandise, templates, and larger print distribution. Check the current agreement and your actual use case rather than assuming one license covers every project.

A Lifetouch watermark may appear on a school-photo preview or on a copy you already saved. The right next step depends on which file you have. A purchased digital photo may be available through your account, while a preview or another person's photo may require permission or a separate purchase.

## Quick Answer

Check your order and account first. If an eligible digital download is available, download it and inspect that file. If you already have a copy that you are authorized to edit, an online image editor may help with a visible mark. Keep the original: editing can change surrounding details, especially in portraits.

| **What you have** | **What to check first** | **Recommended next step** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A watermarked preview | Whether the image is included in a digital order | Check the account or ask about a licensed download |
| A purchased digital photo | Account, selected image and downloaded file | Download the eligible image and inspect the actual file |
| An authorized local copy | Permission, image dimensions and watermark position | Compare an online image editor with the available original |

## Part 1. Identify the Lifetouch Photo You Have

### Preview, Purchased Download, or Authorized Copy?

![Lifetouch album in Shutterfly with download control](/uploads/seo-blog-20261101/how-to-remove-lifetouch-watermark/lifetouch-album.jpg)

A preview lets you see a photograph before deciding what to buy; it is not necessarily the same file as a purchased digital image. Look for the purchase confirmation, the account used for the order, and whether the order includes a digital download. If you received the image from someone else, ask whether you may edit and use it.

Owning a print or purchasing a download does not automatically answer every question about alteration or reuse. Check the applicable license or ask Lifetouch or the photographer when in doubt.

## Part 3. Download or Troubleshoot a Purchased Lifetouch Photo

### Find and Download Your Purchased Photos

#### Step 1. Find Your Purchased Lifetouch Photos

Sign in at Shutterfly using the account associated with the order. According to [Lifetouch’s help page](https://mycollection.lifetouch.com/help), select **Lifetouch** from the account menu to open your personalized page. Confirm that the image is included in your digital order rather than assuming every preview is downloadable.

#### Step 2. Download and Check the Photo

![Lifetouch photo page with Download Selected control highlighted](/uploads/seo-blog-20261101/how-to-remove-lifetouch-watermark/lifetouch-shutterfly-download-selected.jpg)

Select the eligible thumbnail and choose **Download Selected** on the Shutterfly website. Lifetouch also describes an option on that page to download a copyright release for a purchased image. Open the downloaded file itself, rather than relying on a small preview, and check its dimensions and visible marks. Lifetouch notes that downloads from the Shutterfly mobile app are not high resolution. If the photo is missing or the downloaded version is still marked, verify the account and order details and contact the appropriate support team with the order information.

### What to check before you edit a school photo

A watermark crossing a plain backdrop is different from one covering a child’s eye or a name. Zoom in before editing and decide whether the affected detail is important to the final print. For school portraits, also consider who is allowed to upload the image and whether an online service is appropriate for that photo. If you already purchased the digital image, the original download is usually the better comparison point.

## Part 4. Troubleshoot Your Lifetouch Photo Before You Edit

### Your Purchased Photo Is Missing

![Lifetouch student lookup screen showing a no-match message](/uploads/seo-blog-20261101/how-to-remove-lifetouch-watermark/lifetouch-lookup-error.jpg)

Start with the email address used for the order. If you have more than one Shutterfly account, signing into a different account can make a valid purchase look as though it has disappeared. Check these items in order:

1\. Find the order confirmation and verify the email address used for the purchase.

2\. Look for the message that the digital images are ready. Lifetouch says delivery is generally approximately three to five days after an order.

3\. Open the matching account and locate the correct child, event and image. If necessary, use Lifetouch's claiming process or contact support.

After signing in, open the Lifetouch area of your account and look for the correct child, event and image. If you did not receive the delivery email, Lifetouch's help page links to a claiming process using the email address from the purchase. If the photo still does not appear, contact support with the order details and the event information. Avoid posting a child's image or account details publicly when requesting help.

### The Image Still Looks Watermarked After Download

First distinguish the file from its preview. Open the image you actually downloaded from your device's files or photo library, and compare it with the thumbnail in your account. If you opened an older saved copy, download the selected photo again and give the new file a distinct name. Check that the selected image is the one included in your order, not a different pose or a preview from a separate event.

If the downloaded photo itself remains marked, keep the file and record which image and order it came from. Contact Lifetouch or Shutterfly support rather than repeatedly editing the preview. They can check the account and delivery status; an image editor cannot correct an order that has been linked to the wrong account.

### Choose a File That Is Suitable for Printing

A phone preview can be convenient for checking a pose, but image quality becomes more noticeable in a print. Lifetouch says website downloads of the selected digital image are full resolution, while downloads from the Shutterfly mobile app are not high resolution. If you plan to print, compare the dimensions of the saved file with the intended output, and use the full-resolution website download when it is available to you.

Inspect the downloaded file before ordering prints: check the face, background, borders and any text. Keep an untouched copy. If you edit an authorized image to remove a remaining mark, compare both files at the intended print size; a repair that looks smooth on a phone screen can become conspicuous when enlarged.

### Match the Method to the Photo

A small mark on an even studio background is a different editing problem from a transparent mark over hair, glasses or a patterned school uniform. For the former, an online image editor may be practical. For the latter, look for an original image before relying on reconstruction: an editor can produce a plausible patch without recovering the exact detail beneath the mark.

If the photo includes other students, check that you are comfortable uploading it and that you have the appropriate permission. Review the chosen service's current privacy and retention terms, especially when a child's image is involved. For photos where an upload is not appropriate, request an authorized file from the provider or consider an editor you can use under your own data-handling requirements.

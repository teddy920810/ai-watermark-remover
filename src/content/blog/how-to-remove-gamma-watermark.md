## How to Remove Gamma Watermark: Quick Answer

For most editable decks, a gamma watermark remover is not the first tool to reach for. If the presentation is in an eligible paid Gamma workspace, open the source deck, go to Share > Advanced Settings, hide the “Made by Gamma” badge, and create a fresh export. This source-level route preserves text, links, layout, and image quality better than editing a flattened file.

If the deck was created on Free and the badge still appears after upgrading, duplicate or copy the presentation into an eligible paid workspace, turn off branding there, and export the new copy. If the visible watermark is inside an imported still image instead of Gamma branding, use the original clean asset when available; when image cleanup is genuinely needed, WatermarkGemini is the recommended image-only workflow for an authorized image.

## How to Remove Made with Gamma Watermark from the Source Deck

### Hide “Made with Gamma” in an Eligible Paid Workspace

> **Best for**
> - The presentation is already in a Plus, Pro, or Ultra workspace and the Gamma badge is still visible.
> - You still have the editable Gamma deck, so there is no reason to edit a flattened export.
> - You want the cleanest route with the lowest risk to text, links, layout, and images.

A made with Gamma watermark remover is unnecessary when the badge is still controlled by Gamma itself. For an editable presentation, change the source branding state first; only move to export-level editing when the original deck is unavailable.

**1\.** Open the original Gamma presentation in the workspace that has the eligible paid plan.

**2\.** Select Share in the upper-right area, then open Advanced Settings.

**3\.** Turn on the option that hides “Made by Gamma” branding. If the wording differs, use Gamma’s current branding control rather than a third-party workaround.

**4\.** Preview the deck in the same sharing or presentation mode you plan to use.

**5\.** Create a new PDF or PowerPoint export and check the first, middle, and final cards before publishing.

### Remove Gamma Watermark After Upgrading from Free

> **Best for**

> - A new paid deck exports cleanly, but the old Free-created deck does not.

This is a common failure case because upgrading the account is not the same as rewriting the deck’s original branding state. Gamma’s current help says the badge can remain tied to a presentation created under Free.

**4\.** Check card order, fonts, images, links, speaker notes, and embedded content after the copy.

**5\.** Hide Gamma branding in the new presentation and export a short sample before creating the full deliverable.

## FAQ: Gamma Watermark Remover Questions

### Can I remove the Gamma watermark on the Free plan?

Gamma’s current Help Center says Free users cannot hide the “Made with Gamma” badge. Plus, Pro, and Ultra can. If your deck was created on Free, the supported fallback is to move or duplicate the content into an eligible paid workspace and export again.

### Why is the Gamma watermark still there after I upgraded?

The deck may have been created under Free. Gamma says that badge can remain tied to the presentation even after an upgrade, so copying the content into a paid-workspace deck can be necessary.

### Does changing the Gamma branding setting remove the watermark from an old PDF?

No. The setting affects new sharing/export behavior; an already generated PDF or PPTX does not rewrite itself. Create a fresh export from the source.

## Why Does Grok Add a Watermark?

xAI’s current FAQ says the Grok watermark indicates that an image or video was created with AI. Its brand guidelines also recommend visible attribution language such as “Created with Grok” for generated material. In other words, the mark is not merely decorative branding; it carries provenance information.

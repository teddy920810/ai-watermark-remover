To **remove an Adobe Stock watermark** and use an image in a finished project, the most reliable method is to license the asset and download the full-resolution file without the preview mark. If you're already working in Photoshop or Illustrator, you can license a library-linked preview and replace it in your design. A separate online remover is useful only for images you own or are independently authorized to edit; erasing a visible mark does **not** give you an Adobe Stock license. Here's how to choose the right approach without damaging your layout or losing image quality.

## Why Do Adobe Stock Images Have Watermarks?

Adobe Stock watermarks identify selection-stage previews. Designers can place them in mockups before deciding what to license, but a preview is not the final high-resolution asset. According to Adobe's [licensing FAQ](https://helpx.adobe.com/stock/web/common-questions/usage-licensing.html), licensing provides the unmarked file at its highest available resolution. Digitally erasing the preview mark neither improves its source detail nor grants publication rights. Check the license and any **editorial use only** restriction before using the image publicly.

A low-resolution preview is convenient for composition tests, but enlargement or AI repair does not make it equivalent to the licensed original. Keep the Stock asset ID so you can find the correct licensed file later.

## Method 1: Remove the Adobe Stock Watermark by Licensing the Image

To obtain a genuine watermark-free Adobe Stock image, license the asset rather than editing its preview.

### Find and License Your Adobe Stock Image

**Step 1.** Sign in at [Adobe Stock](https://stock.adobe.com/).

**Step 2.** Search for your image or locate its Stock asset ID from the saved preview.

**Step 3.** Open the asset details and check its file type, license terms and any **editorial use only** label.

**Step 4.** Choose the available licensing action and confirm the applicable plan, credit or purchase.

**Step 5.** Download the licensed file from the asset page or license history, not the preview.

![Adobe Stock search results shown in a Creative Cloud Libraries panel.](/uploads/how-to-remove-adobe-stock-watermark/image2.png)

For shared projects, make sure you license the correct asset using the appropriate team account.

Before confirming, check whether the item is a photograph, illustration or vector. The license you need may also depend on whether the image will appear in advertisements, products or editorial content. Record the asset ID, especially when several similar images appear in the same project.

### Download the Watermark-Free High-Resolution Image

After licensing, download the supplied file and check its actual dimensions and format. If already licensed, check Adobe Stock license history before purchasing again. Keep an untouched original and record the Stock ID for future reference. If the download still shows a watermark, verify that it is the licensed file rather than an old preview or cached export.

## Method 3: How to Remove Adobe Stock Watermark in Photoshop

For Adobe Stock previews already placed in a design, Photoshop can replace linked Library assets after you license them. This preserves the layout and gives you the official high-resolution image.

### Add the Adobe Stock Preview to Your Photoshop Library

**Step 1.** Choose **File > Search Adobe Stock** in Photoshop and find your image.

**Step 2.** Select **Save to Library** to retain a watermarked preview in Creative Cloud Libraries.

**Step 3.** Open **Window > Libraries** in Photoshop.

**Step 4.** Find the preview and choose **Place Linked** (or drag it into the design), then position it.

Use a linked Library asset. A flattened screenshot or independent JPEG will not update automatically.

The linked relationship is what allows the licensed asset to replace the preview while retaining its position. If you pasted a screenshot into a layer, you will usually need to replace that copy yourself after licensing.

### License the Image to Replace the Watermarked Preview

**Step 1.** In Photoshop's Libraries panel, right-click the Stock preview.

**Step 2.** Choose **License Image** and confirm the license.

**Step 3.** Let Creative Cloud synchronize the licensed, unmarked file into linked document instances.

**Step 4.** Inspect at full size and save. If the preview remains, try the refresh steps below.

![The License Image command in a Creative Cloud Libraries panel containing Adobe Stock previews.](/uploads/how-to-remove-adobe-stock-watermark/image3.png)

See Adobe's official [Photoshop Stock instructions](https://helpx.adobe.com/photoshop/desktop/create-open-import-images/import-files/import-adobe-stock-assets.html).

Check that you licensed the exact Stock item used in the design. An image that looks similar in search results may have a different asset ID and will not replace the linked item you expected.

## Method 4: How to Remove Adobe Stock Watermark in Illustrator

Illustrator uses Creative Cloud Libraries to preview and license Stock graphics, including assets placed in design layouts.

### License Adobe Stock Assets from the Libraries Panel

**Step 1.** In Illustrator, choose **Window > Libraries**.

**Step 2.** Search for the asset in **Search Adobe Stock**.

**Step 3.** Save a preview with the cloud icon, or select the shopping cart to license the asset directly.

**Step 4.** Confirm **License**; Adobe saves the licensed, watermark-free image to the Library.

**Step 5.** Place the licensed asset and replace any stale copies that did not update.

![Adobe Stock license confirmation dialog shown in the Illustrator guide.](/uploads/how-to-remove-adobe-stock-watermark/image4.png)

See Adobe's [Illustrator Libraries guide](https://helpx.adobe.com/illustrator/desktop/manage-objects/traces-mockups-symbols/add-and-use-graphic-assets-in-libraries.html); panel layout can vary by workspace.

### Why a Copied or Embedded Preview May Stay Watermarked

A copied screenshot or embedded preview may not be linked to the licensed Library source, so it may stay unchanged. Check the object's link and replace unlinked copies with the licensed file. For editable vector paths, download the licensed vector itself; a cleaned raster preview is not equivalent.

## Can You Get Adobe Stock Images Without Paying?

Adobe Stock has a **Free Collection** of selected assets. Confirm that the specific image is free and complete the required licensing step; a free \*preview\* of a paid asset is not a free license. Other assets may require a subscription, credits or a purchase. Always check rights and **editorial use only** restrictions before publishing.

## Why Is an Adobe Stock Watermark Still Visible After Licensing?

Most often the licensed asset and the displayed image are different files. Check the Stock ID and Adobe account, then verify whether the design contains a linked Library item or an old flattened preview. Download the licensed file separately to confirm that it is clean.

A sensible order is to verify the license in your account, open the downloaded file, and only then troubleshoot the design document. If the downloaded file is clean but the layout is not, focus on linked assets, copied previews or stale content rather than trying to remove the text with a retouching brush.

### Fix a Licensed Stock Image That Does Not Update in Photoshop

Adobe describes a specific refresh procedure:

**Step 1.** In the affected Photoshop document, find the Stock layer in **Layers**.

**Step 2.** Right-click the layer and choose **Update Modified Content**.

**Step 3.** Confirm the licensed image and dimensions update. Otherwise, download the licensed file and replace the stale copy.

See Adobe's [Stock troubleshooting guide](https://helpx.adobe.com/photoshop/desktop/troubleshoot/tool-feature-issues/stock-assets-fail-to-update.html).

## Frequently Asked Questions About Adobe Stock Watermarks

### Is it legal to remove an Adobe Stock watermark?

Removing pixels is not the same as obtaining rights. To publish an Adobe Stock asset, license it appropriately and follow the restrictions. You may be able to edit images you independently own or are allowed to modify, subject to relevant obligations. This is not legal advice.

### Can I remove an Adobe Stock watermark for free?

Selected assets in Adobe Stock's Free Collection can be licensed under their terms. Erasing a paid asset's preview watermark with a free tool does not grant a license. For your own permitted images, check the free-processing limits of any cleanup service.

### Can I use Adobe Stock preview images for commercial projects?

Use watermarked previews for evaluation and layout, not as your final commercial file. License the image, download the unmarked original and verify any **editorial use only** restrictions before release.

A mockup used internally to evaluate a layout is different from sending the marked preview as the final client asset or putting it on a public website.

### Are Adobe Stock images copyright-free after licensing?

No. A Stock license grants specified usage rights, not a transfer of copyright or public-domain status. “Royalty-free” does not mean unrestricted. Keep the asset ID and check permitted uses before distribution.

### Can I remove an Adobe Stock watermark from an image without Photoshop?

Yes. Adobe Stock lets you license and download the clean file directly. For a separate JPG or PNG you have permission to edit, an online cleanup tool may help. It does not replace an Adobe Stock license.

### Does WatermarkGemini support Adobe Stock vector files?

Not as native AI, EPS or SVG uploads in its published image workflow; it accepts JPG, PNG and WebP. To preserve editable paths, download the licensed vector from Adobe instead of cleaning a raster preview.

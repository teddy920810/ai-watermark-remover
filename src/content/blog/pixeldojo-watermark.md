PixelDojo does add a watermark to its free daily effects. Its current homepage advertises three free video or photo effects per day with a small watermark, while its dance-generator page says paid plans remove that watermark. Therefore, a general claim that every PixelDojo export is watermark-free would be misleading.

The important question is which workflow you used: a free effect, a particular model’s generation tool, or a paid account’s export. Check that route before paying for a plan or trying to repair the downloaded file. This guide uses PixelDojo’s public pages checked November 14, 2026; it does not claim a hands-on comparison of free and paid account downloads.

A PixelDojo free export watermark, a TikTok attribution link, and invisible AI identification are separate issues. Removing visible pixels does not change the subscription entitlement, the underlying model terms, or an external platform’s disclosure rules.

## Why different PixelDojo pages can seem contradictory

PixelDojo brings multiple image and video models, effects, and creative workflows into one service. A sentence about an individual generation tool is not necessarily a statement about the free daily-effects offer. A public showcase image also does not establish what a free account receives when it downloads a file.

For example, the homepage’s free-effects offer explicitly includes a small watermark. The AI dance video generator similarly explains that free effects carry a watermark and paid plans remove it. Those route-specific statements are stronger evidence for a free dance-effect export than an unspecific claim that AI videos can be created without watermarks.

## How to check a free PixelDojo export

Use a small, non-sensitive input that you own or are authorized to upload. A single test is usually enough to establish whether that workflow’s exported file carries a visible logo; you do not need to generate a whole campaign first.

1.  Open the specific effect page and read the free-use wording. Check the remaining allowance and whether signing in changes the available options.
2.  Choose the effect and supported input. If it animates a photo, use a sharp image with clear subject boundaries and appropriate permission from any identifiable person.
3.  Read the generation or export cost before proceeding. A free daily effect does not make every model, quality setting, or studio operation free.
4.  Download through PixelDojo’s available download control. A screen recording or browser preview is not an equivalent test of the original file.
5.  Play or inspect the complete download locally. Look at corners, opening and closing frames, changing positions, and any end card.
6.  Compare that file with any version uploaded to another service. If a new label appears only after posting, troubleshoot the publishing platform separately.

Keep the original file and generation information until you are satisfied. If a free effect is advertised with a small watermark and the download has one, that is consistent with the published offer; it is not evidence that the download failed.

## What to check before paying for an unwatermarked version

PixelDojo’s published dance-page distinction makes a paid route relevant when you need an unwatermarked effect. However, confirm the exact plan and operation before buying. Do not assume that upgrading removes marks from every old download, changes files already posted elsewhere, or makes all models unlimited.

Check these points together:

- The chosen plan covers the effect or model you actually need.
- The plan’s current watermark statement applies to that output type.
- Your intended resolution, duration, and generation mode are included or clearly priced.
- You understand whether you must download again or regenerate an existing result.
- Your proposed use complies with both PixelDojo’s terms and any applicable model or asset terms.

PixelDojo also describes a Relax mode for selected models and settings. That is a specific queue-based workflow, not a general promise that every model can produce unlimited high-resolution videos without credits. Choosing a paid plan solely from the word “unlimited” can create the wrong expectation.

If the pricing page is unclear about old generations, ask a concrete question: “I made this named effect on the free daily route. Does this plan let me download that existing generation without branding, or must I generate it again?” A precise answer can prevent paying for the wrong operation.

## If a paid export still shows a watermark

Start with identification rather than immediately processing the picture. Is the visible mark PixelDojo branding, an element generated inside the scene, a template’s graphic, or a social platform’s overlay? The remedy depends on that distinction.

First, verify that the correct account and plan are active. Then return to the original generation and use the download route supplied for that plan. Avoid relying on a cached preview, a previously saved free file, or a social repost. Give the downloaded file a new name so you can compare versions reliably.

If branding remains, save the job reference, effect/model name, account-plan information, and a screenshot showing the mark. Contact PixelDojo with those details and ask whether the workflow is covered. Do not repeatedly regenerate at your own expense while guessing which control might change the watermark.

If the mark is actually distorted text generated within a scene, that is an image-quality problem rather than necessarily an export-policy issue. A revised prompt, different input, or authorized retouch may help. None of those methods should be represented as a guaranteed replacement for the provider’s correct unwatermarked export.

## A final file check before sharing

A watermark-free frame alone is not the whole deliverable. Play the complete video to check motion artifacts, face or product consistency, unexpected text, audio, and the ending. Confirm that the file uses the dimensions and duration required for the destination. Keep an untouched master so you can create platform-specific versions without repeatedly downloading compressed copies.

For client work, note the generator, model, generation date, and the rights or permissions relevant to the inputs. A paid subscription and an unwatermarked file do not automatically clear music, another person’s likeness, or a brand asset. Use the actual terms for that workflow rather than treating “commercial use” as a universal clearance statement.

## FAQ

### Does PixelDojo add a watermark to free videos?

Its homepage says the free daily effects include a small watermark. The dance-generator page makes the same free-versus-paid distinction. Check the specific workflow rather than generalizing to all tools.

### Are all PixelDojo paid exports watermark-free?

The dance page says paid plans remove the effect watermark. That does not independently verify every model and export route. Confirm the statement for your chosen workflow and inspect the downloaded file.

### Will upgrading automatically fix a video I already downloaded?

An existing local file will not change by itself. Verify whether PixelDojo allows a new clean download of the old generation or requires another generation.

### Can an image cleanup tool remove a PixelDojo account restriction?

No. Retouching affects image content. Account entitlements, generation charges, and platform labels belong to their respective services.

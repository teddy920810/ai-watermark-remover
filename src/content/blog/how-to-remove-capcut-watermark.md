A CapCut logo at the end of a video is not the same as a watermark printed over the picture. If you still have the editable project, you can often remove a separate ending clip or template layer before exporting. But if the logo is already embedded in a finished MP4, the options are different.

This guide shows how to remove a CapCut watermark on iPhone, Android, PC and Mac; what to check when a template or Pro feature adds branding; and how to clean an authorized exported video without sacrificing the entire frame.

## Quick Answer: How to Remove the CapCut Watermark

**For a CapCut ending watermark:** Open your project, scroll to the last timeline clip, select the CapCut-branded outro, tap or click **Delete**, and export again. This is often free. **For a template corner logo:** check the editable overlay or premium asset first. **For a watermark already embedded in an exported video:** use a clean original if available, or consider an online video cleanup tool such as [WatermarkGemini Video Watermark Remover](https://www.watermarkgemini.com/video-watermark-remover) for a supported video you own or can edit.

## First, Identify Which CapCut Watermark You Have

Before trying a remover, compare the imported source, the CapCut timeline and the exported file. A logo that exists as a separate timeline object should be handled in the project; a mark baked into video pixels may require reconstruction or cropping.

![CapCut web editor timeline showing a selected video clip and editing track](/uploads/how-to-remove-capcut-watermark/image2.png)

| **What you see** | **Most likely cause** | **Best first action** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| CapCut logo only after your last shot | Separate branded ending clip | Delete the outro in the editable project |
| Logo or text over the picture in a template | Editable overlay or branded template asset | Check template, text and sticker tracks |
| No clean export unless you upgrade | Pro asset or restricted export setting | Replace the Pro resource or review the actual offer |
| Logo visible inside a finished video but no project remains | Watermark baked into exported frames | Find the clean original or use an authorized video cleanup method |
| A branded card appended to a finished MP4 | Ending is now part of the exported file | Trim the final seconds if no useful content is lost |

**Tip:** Save an untouched copy before editing. Removing visible branding from a video does not change ownership, licensing or attribution requirements.

## Method 1: Remove the CapCut Ending Watermark for Free

For many projects, this is the fastest solution and does not require AI reconstruction. CapCut's own help explains that template watermarks frequently come from a separate ending segment rather than an unavoidable mark on every exported frame.

### How to Remove the CapCut Ending Watermark on iPhone or Android

1\. Open your existing CapCut project in the mobile app.

2\. Swipe the timeline to the very end and locate the short CapCut-branded clip after your final shot.

3\. Tap the ending clip to select it; make sure you have not selected your actual last scene.

4\. Tap **Delete** or the trash icon. Check that your video's intended final frame remains intact.

5\. Preview the last seconds, export your project and open the newly saved video to confirm the logo is gone.

If you used a template, inspect the overlay and text layers as well; deleting an outro will not remove an unrelated corner logo.

### How to Remove the CapCut Watermark on PC, Mac or Web

1\. Open the editable project in CapCut Desktop on Windows or macOS, or in CapCut Online.

2\. Move the playhead to the end of the timeline and select the separate CapCut ending clip.

3\. Delete that clip. Review other tracks for text, logos, stickers or branded template placeholders.

4\. Export a new file and examine both the ending and the frames where the logo previously appeared.

The position and availability of controls can vary between versions. Desktop and web template libraries also differ from the mobile experience, so use the assets actually available in your project.

## Method 2: Remove a CapCut Template Watermark or Corner Logo

If you searched **how to remove CapCut watermark from template**, first determine whether the logo is an editable object or already part of the underlying media. Removing the wrong layer may damage the project without changing the final watermark.

### Check Editable Template Layers Before Export

1\. Open the template project and scrub to a frame where the watermark appears.

2\. Inspect overlay, text, sticker and branding tracks for a selectable logo or label.

3\. If permitted by the template's terms, remove or replace the editable element, then preview the same frame again.

4\. Check the export window for Pro-tagged assets or restrictions before saving.

If the watermark remains even when every visible logo layer is hidden, check whether it is present in your imported source clip. Some templates or assets may not be editable in your current app or region.

### Does CapCut Pro Remove Watermarks?

Sometimes, but not in every situation. Certain premium templates, effects, music, fonts and export settings can trigger a CapCut Pro requirement. If a prompt appears, identify which item is restricted and either replace it with an available free alternative or use the entitlement required for that feature. Subscribing will not erase a watermark already printed into an unrelated source video.

## Method 4: Remove a CapCut Watermark from Video for Free Without Pro

If you are looking for **remove CapCut watermark online free**, start with methods that do not involve paid processing: delete a separate ending clip, replace a free-template overlay, re-export from your own clean project or trim an already exported outro. These options are different from AI erasing a logo burned into the picture.

### Trim a CapCut Ending from an Already Exported Video

If the CapCut logo appears only in a standalone end card, open a copy of the exported video in a video editor or your phone's trim controls. Move the ending boundary to the first frame of the unwanted branded card, trim that portion and save a new file. Watch the new ending to make sure speech, music and your credits were not cut off. **Trimming cannot remove a logo that overlaps footage you want to keep.**

### Use CapCut's AI Remove or Crop Tools for a Logo Inside the Frame

On supported desktop builds, CapCut documents an **AI remove** control under **Video > Basic**. Import your own video, select the clip, brush the visible unwanted area, run Remove and inspect the result. Availability, restrictions and pricing may depend on your version and account. A crop can also hide a logo on an expendable edge, but it removes part of the frame and can change the composition.

## FAQs About Removing CapCut Watermarks

### Can You Remove a CapCut Watermark for Free?

Yes, if the mark is a separate ending clip or an editable template element that you are allowed to remove, you can often delete it in CapCut and export again without paying. A Pro-restricted export or AI processing workflow may have a charge. WatermarkGemini video processing currently uses allocated credits rather than the free image allowance.

### How Do I Remove the CapCut Watermark from a Template?

Open the template project, delete a separate CapCut ending clip and check the overlay, text and sticker tracks for editable branding. Then inspect export restrictions and re-export. If the logo is part of an uneditable source asset, deleting the ending alone will not erase it.

### How Do I Remove a CapCut Watermark in the Corner Without Cropping?

If the logo is a selectable overlay, delete that layer in the original project. If it is baked into an exported video you own, an AI reconstruction tool can attempt to replace the visible region while preserving the frame. Expect results to vary with background movement and detail, and review the returned clip carefully.

### How Do I Remove the CapCut Watermark on PC or Mac?

Open CapCut Desktop, select the separate branded outro at the end of the timeline and delete it. Check editable template layers before exporting. For an already embedded watermark, supported CapCut desktop versions also document **Video > Basic > AI remove**, subject to your actual app version and entitlement.

### Does CapCut Pro Remove the Watermark Automatically?

Not from every file. CapCut Pro may unlock eligible premium assets or clean-export settings. It does not automatically remove a logo already burned into a source video, and a separate outro may be removable without Pro in the first place.

### Can I Remove a CapCut Ending Watermark from a Downloaded Video?

If the logo is only a separate branded end card, trim those last seconds from a copy of the video. If the CapCut mark overlaps footage you need to keep, trimming will remove valuable content; try obtaining the clean source or use a compatible cleanup workflow instead.

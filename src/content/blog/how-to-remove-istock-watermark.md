For an iStock stock-photo preview, download the properly licensed, watermark-free original instead of editing the preview. If a visible overlay remains on an image you own or have explicit permission to modify, an online cleanup tool can help—but it cannot grant a stock-photo license or recover details that were never in the preview.

## Method 1: Get the iStock Original Without a Watermark

This is the recommended route when you want to remove the iStock watermark from a stock image for a website, presentation, advertisement, or other finished project. The licensed download—not a repaired preview—provides the authentic source file.

![Illustrative scene: An illustrative image purchase and download screen on a desktop monitor. Not a screenshot of the named software or evidence of a completed product test.](/uploads/how-to-remove-istock-watermark/image3.jpeg)

**1\.** Find the image on iStock. Open its asset page, or use the asset ID if you recorded it while viewing the preview.

**2\.** Check the proposed use. Confirm that the image is suitable for your project and whether the standard license is enough. Certain uses, such as some merchandise or high-volume reproduction, may require extended rights.

**3\.** Download the licensed image. Sign in, select the available file size and follow the credit or subscription checkout flow shown for that asset.

**4\.** Verify the result. Open the downloaded file at full size, confirm the iStock preview mark is absent, and store the asset ID and license record with your project.

### Already licensed the image? Check download history first

If you paid for a file but still see a watermark, you may be opening the free comp saved earlier. Sign in using the original purchasing account and find the file in download history. iStock says credit-purchased files can be downloaded again from their history; subscription redownload access depends on the subscription remaining active. If you cannot retrieve the asset, use iStock support with the asset ID and purchase details.

An AI cleanup result is not the same thing as an original, watermark-free stock file. Watermarks that cross complex details may force the system to invent visual information. Use these checks before publishing an otherwise authorized edit:

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Why does my iStock image still have a watermark after I paid?

You may have saved a watermarked comp instead of the licensed download. Check the account that purchased the asset, identify the matching asset ID, and download the licensed file from your history if your plan permits. Contact iStock support if the clean file is missing.

### Can I remove an iStock watermark online for free without Photoshop?

You can use an online image-cleanup service on content you own or are explicitly allowed to modify. WatermarkGemini offers eligible standard image uses, but processing requires Google sign-in and may involve credits. It does not authorize use of a free iStock comp as a finished image.

### Can I remove a watermark from an iStock image on my phone?

WatermarkGemini runs in a browser, so a compatible mobile browser may be able to open its workspace. Actual interface behavior depends on the device. You still need a supported file, a Google sign-in, and permission to edit; the safest approach for an iStock stock photo is to download the licensed original.

### Will AI watermark removal restore the original iStock photo?

No. It estimates missing or obscured pixels and can alter detail, especially under large logos and repeating marks. A licensed iStock download provides the genuine file and is preferable for high-resolution professional use.

### Can I edit an iStock photo with AI after licensing it?

iStock permits edits to many licensed creative photos and illustrations, including certain AI modifications, subject to its license and restrictions. “Editorial use only” files have different rules and generally should not be altered. Always check the individual asset terms.

### Is it legal to remove the iStock watermark from a preview image?

iStock expressly limits watermarked comps to temporary test or sample layouts and prohibits their use in final or publicly available materials. Removing the overlay does not convey a license. Obtain the appropriate license or direct permission before using someone else’s work.

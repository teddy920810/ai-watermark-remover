If you need to **remove a Bandicam watermark** from a video, first check which Bandicam version created it and whether the clean original recording still exists. In Bandicam 2027, a free recording has no watermark, but exporting an edited video or burned-in subtitles with the free version adds one. Older free releases can burn a Bandicam mark into the recording itself. The cleanest fix is to use an unmarked original or re-export after activating a license. If neither is possible and you own the footage or have permission to edit it, an online video cleanup tool may help.

## Why Does Bandicam Add a Watermark? Check Your Version First

Watermarks depend on the version and where the video came from. Check whether the mark was recorded into the original, added during editing, or displayed only by your video player.

A useful test is to locate the recording produced before editing. Compare that file with the copy uploaded to YouTube or sent to a client. If only the edited version contains the mark, buying an AI remover may be unnecessary.

Bandicam's [official guide](https://www.bandicam.com/how-to-remove-bandicam-watermark/) and [version comparison](https://www.bandicam.com/faqs/free-full-version/) explain the difference. The 2027 export mark often appears near the center; older recording marks appear at the top. Verify the actual file rather than relying on position alone.

### Is the Mark Part of Your Video or Just the Player Display?

Play the MP4 in two players. If text appears in only one, it may be a filename overlay. If it stays in an exported frame, it's embedded in the video. Also check Bandicam's **Add logo overlay to video** option. Disabling a custom logo affects future recordings, not saved files.

For a more reliable check, pause on a frame where the text is visible and capture a screenshot. Open the original file on another computer or player. A name that vanishes when playback begins is especially likely to be an interface label, not recorded content. Keep the untouched file before making changes.

## How to Stop Bandicam Watermarks in Future Recordings or Exports

Preventing a mark on a new export is different from fixing one already burned into a file.

### Activate Bandicam to Export Videos Without a Watermark

Bandicam 2027 free recordings are unmarked within its five-minute limit. To export edited footage without a watermark:

**Step 1.** Check **UNREGISTERED** or **Settings > About** in Bandicam.

**Step 2.** With a valid license, select **About > Activate** and finish activation.

**Step 3.** Open the original recording, reapply any edits and export again.

**Step 4.** Play the new export. If marked, verify activation and contact Bandicam support.

![Bandicam activation window with the Activate License option.](/uploads/how-to-remove-bandicam-watermark/image2.png)

Check the [official license comparison](https://www.bandicam.com/faqs/free-full-version/) for current permissions and paid features.

A license applies to the software workflow, not to previously rendered files. Keep a copy of the unedited recording so you can replace an export later without repeating the capture. If your work is commercial, check which Bandicam license type permits that use.

### Why Buying Bandicam Does Not Clean Previously Saved Videos

Activation cannot erase marks already rendered into an MP4. Re-export a Bandicam 2027 original under the active license. For an older free recording with the mark baked in, locate an unmarked source or consider an authorized repair tool.

### Crop or Cover a Watermark: Free but with Trade-Offs

For footage you're allowed to edit, cropping can remove a top-edge mark but may also cut off menus or change the aspect ratio. Covering or blurring preserves dimensions but leaves a visible patch. Review the entire clip: a subject may move into the affected area later.

To judge a crop, preview the resulting frame at the final destination's aspect ratio rather than assuming the new composition will fit. For example, cutting away the top of a desktop recording may remove window titles that establish which app is being demonstrated. A solid overlay is faster, but the viewer will still see a changed region.

## Frequently Asked Questions About Bandicam Watermarks

### Can I remove a Bandicam watermark for free from a saved video?

Yes, if a clean original exists. In Bandicam 2027, the original free recording is watermark-free; locate it instead of the edited export. Cropping or covering a marked file may also cost nothing, but alters the image. AI cleanup can require credits.

If you have only the old watermarked file, there is no guaranteed free fix that both preserves every pixel and recovers the original hidden detail.

### Does the free version of Bandicam still add a watermark?

Bandicam 2027 free **recordings** have no watermark, but free edited or burned-in subtitle **exports** can. Older free releases marked the recordings themselves. Check which version created the file.

### Why do I still see a watermark after buying Bandicam?

It may be burned into an older file. Activate your license under **About > Activate**, then export again from the source. Also check for custom logos and player filename labels. Contact Bandicam support if a new licensed export remains marked.

### What is the difference between Bandicam and Bandicut watermarks?

Bandicam records the screen and may mark certain free exports; Bandicut is separate cutting/joining software whose free exports can have their own watermark. Activating one product does not retroactively change files created by the other. See the [Bandicut FAQ](https://www.bandicam.com/bandicut-video-cutter/support/faq/bandicut-watermark/).

Check which application produced the final file before changing the license settings, since the two products have distinct export workflows.

### Can I remove a Bandicam watermark without cropping the video?

An AI video remover may reconstruct a marked area without changing frame dimensions, if you have editing rights. Motion and small text can still produce artifacts. An unmarked original or licensed re-export is more reliable.

### Can WatermarkGemini process AVI or MOV Bandicam recordings?

Its published video workflow supports MP4, not direct AVI or MOV input. Convert a permitted source into a compatible MP4 and verify the frame rate, length, file size, picture and audio before submitting.

To remove a Canva watermark, open the original design and find the element causing it. License the premium content through the available purchase or plan options, or replace it with a free element or an upload you own. If you already paid, check the account and team used for the purchase before paying again.

There is another possibility: the visible mark may be your own logo or text layer rather than Canva's premium-content watermark. That can often be deleted directly in the editable design. Distinguishing these situations saves time and avoids damaging a finished image with an eraser when the underlying problem is account access or an editable overlay.

## Quick answer by watermark type

| **What you see** | **Best first action** | **Expected result** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Premium element watermark | License or replace that element | A clean authorized design export |
| Watermark despite an active paid plan | Check account and team access | Correct entitlement recognized |
| Your own added logo or draft text | Delete or hide that layer | The design remains editable |
| Mark already flattened into your own upload | Recover the unmarked source or repair a copy | A new raster image, not editable layers |
| Canva website branding | Check website publishing and plan options | A site setting rather than photo repair |

## Part 1 Identify which element creates the watermark

Open the original Canva project rather than starting from a downloaded JPG. Select the marked element and check whether Canva identifies it as premium. A design can contain several paid photos, videos, illustrations, or other elements, so removing one does not necessarily clear every marked page.

![A separately selectable text element in a Canva template. The selected element is editable; the view does not show a premium-content license.](/uploads/blog-20261118/how-to-remove-canva-watermark/figure-1.jpg)

*Figure 1. A separately selectable text element in a Canva template. The selected element is editable; the view does not show a premium-content license. [Source](https://guides.lib.uoguelph.ca/Canva)*

Inspect every page you plan to export. A free template can still contain a premium element added later, and a mostly original design can include one paid decorative item. Check backgrounds and overlapping graphics too; the obvious central photograph may not be the only source of the mark.

If the watermark arrived inside an uploaded image, Canva may not control it at all. A supplier's stock-preview watermark, your old brand logo, and a text annotation you exported previously are different cases. Trace the image back to its source and look for an authorized clean copy before attempting pixel repair.

Finally, distinguish a photo watermark from website branding. If the issue appears only after publishing a Canva website, changing a JPG will not alter the hosted site's footer or branding controls. Work in the website publishing settings and current plan conditions instead.

## Part 2 Remove premium-content watermarks in the project

### Way 1 Use an eligible license or plan

Canva's help page describes purchasing premium elements through downloading or an element's Remove watermarks option, alongside upgrading to an eligible plan. The choices available to your account may vary. Check the actual download screen before assuming that a particular one-off purchase is offered everywhere.

1.  Open the design and select a premium element that displays a watermark. Confirm that it is the intended asset and not an uploaded preview from another provider.
2.  Use the available Remove watermarks option or proceed to the design's download flow to review licensing choices. Read the purchase scope, account, and total before accepting a paid option.
3.  If using an existing plan, confirm that the current account and team have the necessary access. Switching teams can change which benefits apply to the same visible design.
4.  After the entitlement is recognized, return to the design and inspect all marked elements. A purchase covering one element or one design should not be treated as unrestricted reuse everywhere.
5.  Download the intended pages in the appropriate format. Open the actual saved file and check the marked areas, rather than relying only on the editor preview.

This route preserves the original asset and avoids reconstructing detail beneath a watermark. It is especially useful for a design whose photograph or illustration cannot easily be replaced without changing the layout.

### Way 2 Replace premium elements with permitted free content

If you need to remove a Canva watermark for free, use elements available under the free options for your account or upload content you own. This changes the design's ingredients instead of trying to bypass premium access.

![A permitted replacement image can be added from Uploads. This university tutorial illustrates placing an uploaded asset on the canvas.](/uploads/blog-20261118/how-to-remove-canva-watermark/figure-2.jpg)

*Figure 2. A permitted replacement image can be added from Uploads. This university tutorial illustrates placing an uploaded asset on the canvas. [Source](https://guides.lib.uoguelph.ca/Canva)*

1.  Duplicate the design so the original arrangement remains available. Note the marked element's role, such as a background photo, icon, or texture.
2.  Find a suitable free replacement or upload your own image. Use the available search and filtering options, and inspect the replacement's status before inserting it.
3.  Replace the premium element. Recheck the crop, frame fit, color contrast, and text wrapping because a new asset may have a different shape or visual weight.
4.  Repeat for the remaining marked elements on every export page. Do not stop after fixing the first page if the download includes several.
5.  Download a test copy and reopen it. Confirm that all desired content remains and that the chosen free route permits the intended output.

A replacement is often preferable when the marked element is decorative. For a distinctive stock photograph central to the design, a licensed download may save substantial redesign work. Make the choice based on the finished layout, not only the presence of an upgrade prompt.

## Part 3 Fix watermarks that remain after payment

Canva identifies the wrong account or team as a possible reason for paid users still seeing watermarks. Check the email used for the purchase and the team shown on the invoice. A browser can contain several signed-in identities, and the correct design link alone does not prove the correct entitlement is active.

For a previously purchased premium element, Canva's help also distinguishes reuse within the purchased design from using the element in a different design. A purchase history entry is useful evidence, but its license scope matters. Review that scope before assuming the platform has charged you twice for an identical right.

If access still looks wrong, save the design link, purchase reference, account email, and a screenshot of the marked element for Canva support. Avoid repeatedly purchasing the same item while the account mismatch is unresolved. A local eraser cannot correct a billing or entitlement error in the project.

Canva Print proofs create another distinction. The help page explains that a proof can display premium-content watermarks before the relevant checkout is complete, while the actual print order can differ after payment. Assess the proof within the print process instead of treating it as a failed ordinary image download.

## Part 4 Delete a watermark you added yourself

If your own logo or draft label is a separate text or image element, select it and delete it in the editable project. Unlock the item or enter its group if necessary, then check that you did not remove the underlying photo. Duplicate the design before making changes when you need both branded and unbranded versions.

If you cannot select the mark independently, it may be baked into an uploaded image. Find the original in your files, camera library, or earlier project exports. Replacing that one upload with the unmarked source usually preserves more detail than any restoration tool can.

For teams, keep the master design and approved logo file together. A clean master, a branded derivative, and a clearly named delivery copy make future updates much easier. Repeatedly downloading, erasing, and reuploading finished files discards the editing flexibility that Canva provides.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I remove Canva watermarks without Canva Pro

Yes, by replacing premium content with permitted free elements or your own uploads. Where offered, a specific premium-content purchase may also be available. Check the actual choices for your account and design.

### Why does a free icon appear to have a watermark

Check the selected asset's status and source. It may be a premium item, a paid part of a template, or an uploaded preview. Find the element causing the mark before choosing a solution.

### Does erasing the mark unlock the premium element

No. Editing visible pixels does not change an asset's entitlement or license. Resolve access through Canva or replace the content.

### Can an image eraser remove Canva website branding

No. Website branding belongs to the published site's settings and plan behavior. Work in the website publishing controls; altering a photo cannot change the hosted footer.

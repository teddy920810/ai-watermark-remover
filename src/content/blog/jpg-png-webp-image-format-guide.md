The right image format depends on the content, transparency requirements, and where the result will be used. Choosing well helps preserve quality without creating unnecessarily large files.

## JPG for photographs

JPG is widely compatible and efficient for photos. Because it uses lossy compression, repeated editing and saving can introduce visible artifacts.

## PNG for transparency

PNG supports transparent backgrounds and sharp graphic edges. It is a strong choice for logos and interface assets, but photographs may produce larger files.

## WEBP for the web

WEBP offers modern compression and can support transparency. It is often a good delivery format for websites when browser compatibility is not a concern.
